# Academic Profile

Prof. Zhang develops artificial intelligence, geometric analysis, and shape-analysis methods for medical image enhancement, segmentation, registration, and biomarker extraction. His work focuses on vascular and small-vessel diseases across the eye, brain, and other organs, connecting methodological innovation with clinically meaningful imaging markers.

Before joining Nanjing University, he was a professor at the Chinese Academy of Sciences in Ningbo. He received his Ph.D. from the IMAG/e group at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) in 2017, continued as a postdoctoral researcher at TU/e, and in 2018 joined the Laboratory of Neuro Imaging at the Keck School of Medicine, University of Southern California.

# Research Focus

01

Medical image intelligence

Image enhancement, pathology segmentation and detection, image registration, foundation models, multimodal learning, and medical agents.

02

Vascular analysis

2D and 3D retinal microvascular segmentation, neurovascular image analysis, and quantitative vessel biomarker extraction.

03

Geometry and shape

Vascular and cortical surface reconstruction, topology-aware modeling, intrinsic geometry, and interpretable shape analysis.

04

Clinical translation

Research on cerebrovascular and small-vessel disease, diabetic retinopathy and neuropathy, AMD, glaucoma, Alzheimer’s disease, stroke, and moyamoya disease.
