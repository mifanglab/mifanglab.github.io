# Join MI² Lab

Master’s · Ph.D. · Research Assistant · Internship

Research grounded in real medical questions

We welcome students and researchers interested in medical imaging, computer vision, and medicine–engineering collaboration. Our work starts from concrete questions and emphasizes method design, careful experiments, and clinical collaboration.

Application details

# Research and Training

Research practice

Work through literature review, method design, experimental analysis, and scientific writing around a well-defined question.

Clinical collaboration

Study medical images and clinical data with hospital and research partners, from problem formulation to validation.

Exchange and publication

Participate in open discussion, code development, and academic exchange, with support for publishing in medical imaging and AI venues.

# Who We Are Looking For

  • Applicants for master’s, regular Ph.D., or direct Ph.D. programs at Nanjing University.
  • Background in computer science, biomedical engineering, medical imaging, clinical medicine, applied mathematics, or a related discipline.
  • Clear interest in medical imaging, computer vision, multimodal large models, medical agents, or medicine–engineering research.
  • Strong programming and mathematical foundations; publication or open-source experience is a plus.
  • Reliable, collaborative, proactive in communication, and ready for sustained research.

Other ways to work with us

Research assistants, interns, visiting students, and collaborators from hospitals, imaging centers, and life-science research groups are also welcome to get in touch.
